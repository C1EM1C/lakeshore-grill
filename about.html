Our story

Good food, no shortcuts

Lakeshore Grill began as a simple question: why should a quick lunch taste rushed? We opened a small kitchen on Lakeshore Road to serve students, teachers and neighbours food we would happily eat ourselves.

Our patties are smashed on a hot grill, our fries are cut in house and our maple comes from Quebec producers. The menu is short on purpose, so every dish gets full attention.

Come as you are, bring a friend and stay as long as you like.
